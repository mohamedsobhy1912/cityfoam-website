## 领域智识地图

### 专家心智模型
1. Learn chunks, not single words ("make a decision", not make + decision)
2. Comprehensible input drives acquisition; rules accelerate noticing
3. English is stress-timed: meaning lives in stressed words; unstressed ones shrink
4. Stop translating from Arabic: English needs a verb (is/are) and fixed SVO order
5. Output + feedback: speaking/writing early exposes gaps

### 争议地图
- Explicit grammar vs pure input (consensus: both, input-heavy)
- Fluency first vs accuracy first
- British vs American model (consensus: pick one for production, understand both)

### 诊断性问题集
1. Why is "He student" wrong but natural to an Arabic speaker?
2. "I am working here since 2020" — what's wrong and why?
3. a/the: "I bought car. Car is red." fix + rule
4. Difference: "I go" vs "I'm going"
5. Why does "What do you want to do?" sound like "Whaddaya wanna do?"
6. "Did you went?" — fix + rule
7. When is "will" vs "going to" right?
8. "I have seen him yesterday" — fix
9. Polite request at work: 3 levels of politeness
10. "If I will have time, I call you" — fix
